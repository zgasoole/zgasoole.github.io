Research line 03

Spatial Evidence and Decision Methods

Research on how multi-source spatial data can be translated into evidence for environmental pressure, spatial provision, spatial mismatch, and planning priorities.

Scale: neighbourhood · street · city

Featured project: Guangzhou Hot-Humid Active-Health

Analysis chain

The featured project connects environmental conditions, spatial provision, local and neighbouring relationships, and priority screening.

Methods

Current method family

GIS, remote sensing, POI and OSM integration, spatial statistics, accessibility analysis, simulation, indicator design, and mixed methods.

Evidence boundary

Interpretation limits

The analysis identifies relative spatial patterns at street scale. Interpretation distinguishes environmental pressure from actual demand, spatial provision from observed use, and spatial association from causal effect.

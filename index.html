Architecture · Urban Studies · Spatial Evidence

Zhang Yi

I study how environmental pressures, spatial provision, and institutional arrangements shape urban regeneration and everyday health environments.

My work combines architectural inquiry with GIS, remote sensing, spatial statistics, and mixed methods. It has developed from functional environments for differentiated users toward neighbourhood transformation and city-scale spatial evidence.

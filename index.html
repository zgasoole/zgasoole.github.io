Architecture · Urban Studies · Spatial Evidence

Zhang Yi

Researcher · Educator

Research on how environmental pressures, spatial provision, and institutional arrangements shape urban regeneration and everyday health environments.

Architectural inquiry is combined with GIS, remote sensing, spatial statistics, and mixed methods. The research trajectory extends from functional environments for differentiated users to place-based transformation and city-scale spatial decision support.

Three connected research lines span building-scale environments, place-based transformation, and city-scale spatial evidence.

Projects and publications are linked through the relevant research line.

Research line 02

Place-based Spatial Transformation

Research on how local spatial resources, cultural continuity, urban morphology, social structure, and institutional arrangements shape neighbourhood, rural, and heritage transformation.

Scale: place · neighbourhood · district

Focus: regeneration · governance · spatial assets

Selected themes

The line connects spatial design, local context, and implementation conditions.

Neighbourhood

Urban acupuncture and restructuring

Small-scale intervention, neighbourhood transformation, public-space restructuring, and relationships between local actions and wider spatial change.

Lingnan contexts

Rural renewal and cultural continuity

Traditional craftsmanship, village resilience, local cultural resources, and adaptive renewal in Lingnan settings.

Adaptive transformation

Heritage and existing spatial assets

Industrial heritage, coastal districts, existing buildings, and the reuse of spatial resources under changing urban conditions.

Research extensions

Institutional incentives, implementation capacity, coordination, and bargaining conditions in regeneration.
